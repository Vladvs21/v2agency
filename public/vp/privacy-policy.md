# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how our automation service ("Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you connect and manage your social media accounts across supported platforms, including Meta (Instagram, Threads, Facebook), TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and Reddit.

### 1. Information We Collect
When you authorize the Application via official OAuth protocols, we access:
- **Authentication Credentials:** Encrypted OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and account identifiers necessary to authenticate publishing requests on your behalf.
- **Account Metadata:** Public account information (username, channel/page ID, profile image) strictly used to display connection status inside your dashboard.
- **Content Data:** Images, videos, captions, and text assets provided or scheduled by you for publishing.

We **do not** collect, store, or process user passwords or financial details.

### 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Publish, schedule, and format user-approved social media posts across selected platforms.
- Retrieve publishing status, rate-limit consumption, and error logs directly related to scheduled content.
- Refresh expired session tokens to maintain continuous automated workflows.

### 3. Data Storage and Security
All authorization tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your account details or credentials to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

### 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party APIs
Our Application interacts directly with official developer APIs:
- **Meta Platform APIs:** Subject to the Meta Privacy Policy.
- **TikTok API:** Subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy.
- **Google / YouTube API Services:** Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
- **Pinterest & Reddit APIs:** Governed by their respective developer and platform terms.

### 5. Data Deletion and Revocation
You can revoke the Application's access at any time directly through the security/permissions settings of each connected platform. To request the complete removal of your stored tokens, logs, and metadata from our servers, send a deletion request to: **vp.social.grw@gmail.com**. All associated records will be purged within 48 hours.
